Try the mortgage example

Start with 2ND +/− ENTER to reset.

  1. Set monthly payments: 2ND I/Y, type 12, ENTER, then 2ND CPT.
  2. Enter 360 N, 75000 PV, 425.84 +/− PMT.
  3. Press CPT I/Y. The result is 5.50%.
Keyboard shortcuts

Use number keys, + − * /, parentheses, and the decimal point. = calculates; Enter enters a worksheet value or calculates outside a worksheet. Backspace corrects a digit; Esc is CE/C. Arrow keys move through worksheets. Press F2 for 2ND.

Undo and calculation history

Tap the left side of the screen, up to the center of ENTER, to undo one calculator action. Ctrl+Z (or Command+Z) does the same. Undo keeps the last 100 actions while this page is open, including entries, clears, pastes, and reopened calculations.

Tap the middle of the screen, from ENTER’s center to the right edge of IRR, for History. The last 50 completed calculations save in this browser with their full stored results, inputs, and settings. Reopen restores the saved calculator session, including registers and worksheets. Undo returns to your previous work. History begins with calculations made after this feature was added; reloading preserves history but clears Undo.

TVM reminders, copy, and themes

Entering any TVM value reveals all five registers. Enter an explicit zero when it is an input. With four inputs entered, the remaining variable shows ?. Computed answers are marked with an asterisk.

Tap the gold Copy key to copy the full stored value. Hold it to paste the last value copied here into the active entry; press ENTER to store a worksheet entry. With the key focused, Shift+Enter also pastes.

Hold the upper-right corner to turn the calculator over and customize its colors. Keyboard users can focus it and press Enter.
